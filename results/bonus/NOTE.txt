Bonus results in this folder (gil-on.csv, gil-off.csv, the natural race and the timeline files) were produced on our laptop: 4 cores, Windows 11, CPython 3.13.15 standard build and CPython 3.13.18 free-threaded build, 16 MiB generated input, median of 5 runs.
Exact times differ from machine to machine; the pattern (no thread speedup with the GIL on, real speedup and lost updates with the GIL off) does not.
